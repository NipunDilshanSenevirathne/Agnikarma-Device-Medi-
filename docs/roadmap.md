# Remaining work to a finished product

1. Confirm first market, intended use, trained users and the regulatory classification/jurisdiction. Define a narrow first clinical indication.
2. Review P3 wiring and firmware independently; construct guarded bench units; execute HIL and fault tests. Resolve common-cause failures and sensor/relay timing with measured energy budgets.
3. Qualify cartridge, brass material/process, dual-sensor retention, electrical insulation, identity/lock and validated cleaning/sterilization. Define clinical exposure limits through the approved programme.
4. Select and qualify a protected battery pack/charger. Complete charging lockout, current sensing if required, power/thermal design and whole-system tests.
5. Design the compact integrated board and mechanical housing around measured requirements. The low-current carrier routing/native checks and fabrication candidates are now delivered; complete independent CAM/physical review, compact integrated electronics, manufacturing drawings, tolerances and process validation.
6. Validate firmware in target hardware, requirements traceability, usability, electrical safety/EMC, biocompatibility, reprocessing, durability and clinical performance.
7. Complete quality/regulatory release, controlled production, service/calibration, traceability, complaint handling and post-market monitoring.

No schedule or budget is promised without supplier/laboratory quotations and a scoped clinical/regulatory plan.
