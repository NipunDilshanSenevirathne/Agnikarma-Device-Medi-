# Images and drawing provenance - revision 0.5.0, 31 October 2026

The AI illustrations are retained as appearance/bench equipment references. Version 0.5.0 adds native PCB and mechanical images from the actual supplied CAD. Sensor and terminal drawings were regenerated from the expanded electrical records; the new `sensor-wiring` SVG/PNG shows the two separate four-wire elements. Images are not evidence of a manufactured or tested assembly.

- `product-concept.png`: photorealistic AI edit of the handheld illustration, with exactly five preset buttons and the five requested screen values. Future handheld packaging, not manufactured hardware. Exact prompt is saved.
- `modular-bench-concept.png`: photorealistic AI equipment illustration constrained to the P3 parts list, with five presets. The exact prompt lists both Pico modules, both MAX31865 boards, Pololu supply, Omron relay, TC4420 driver, IRLZ44N, PN2222A, passives and the intended wiring. Rendered component geometry and individual leads are approximate; the picture cannot establish electrical correctness. No physical equipment has been photographed. Exact prompt is saved.
- `terminal-wiring`: deterministic diagram generated from hardware/design.json by tools/wiring_drawing.py; same net labels denote connection. It is the electrical record, together with the CSVs, rather than the AI image. Physical verification remains pending.
- `architecture`, `power-stage`, `brass-tips`: deterministic SVG/PNG diagrams generated by tools/regenerate_engineering.py.
- `carrier-top-native.png` / `carrier-bottom-native.png`: actual KiCad 10.0.8 raytraces of the supplied bare carrier. No components or physical test evidence are implied. `carrier-top.svg`, `carrier-bottom.svg` and `carrier-routing.svg` are native layer plots.
- `controller-assembly-native.png` / `tip-family-native.png`: native OpenSCAD source renders, when present. The enclosure view is a controller-only fit model; contact-end shapes contain no heater/lock internals.

Image screens show a preset-selection presentation: 50, 100, 150, 200 and 250 C. During operation the firmware also shows measured temperature and faults; that monitoring has not been removed.
