# Custom cartridge procurement / validation specification

Preferred function: heat a defined brass contact surface from a low-voltage battery while electrically insulating heater and sensors from exposed metal. One interchangeable cartridge at a time; positive locking and a two-channel interlock. The prototype power calculations assume 2.2 ohm nominal resistance, tolerance +/-10%; any actual heater must be measured and reviewed before connecting.

Required supplier deliverables: alloy certificate and impurity analysis; finished part drawing and surface finish; heater material/geometry and cold/hot resistance; insulation construction and dielectric evidence; two independently retained Pt1000 elements with lead specification; joining/sealing materials; measured sensor-to-face mapping; thermal cycling and reprocessing compatibility; manufacturing and traceability process; electrical identity and lock sequence.

No wire-alloy/gauge/winding-length recipe is released. Selecting a nominal resistance alone cannot establish wire operating temperature, dielectric robustness, hotspot control, thermal expansion or sterilization suitability. Commission the supplier to propose and test the construction. The bench resistor is not a medical heater or sterile tip.

Brass candidate CuZn30 is not approved by this project. Do not substitute leaded free-machining C36000. Finished-device chemical/biological evaluation must cover machining residue, oxidation, corrosion, joining materials and end-of-life reprocessing. A plated surface introduces wear and adhesion risks.

Acceptance criteria for clinical exposure, accuracy, overshoot, contact duration/force, lesion effects and safe handling must come from the approved preclinical/clinical programme. Supplier data do not replace finished-device testing.
